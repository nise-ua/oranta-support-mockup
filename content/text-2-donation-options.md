# Donation Options

We are grateful for every contribution and strive to make donating as convenient as possible. Oranta currently accepts donations through multiple methods:

- Zelle
- PayPal
- Personal or business checks payable
- Cash donations

In addition, we are currently working on establishing options for direct bank transfers, which hopefully will be available soon. No matter how you choose to give, your support helps us provide medical aid, humanitarian assistance, and animal rescue services where they are needed most. Thank you once again for your generosity, trust, and belief in our mission. Together, we are making a difference.
